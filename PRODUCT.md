# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners and business owners across the Phoenix metro area visit Clear Impression Services to find cleaning services, check service coverage, and request a quote.

## Product Purpose

Help visitors understand window, solar panel, and screen cleaning services and reach the company through the quote form, phone, or text.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Explain available services and areas through the homepage, service pages, and city pages.
- Use the existing reviews, FAQ, and appointment terms to answer questions before visitors contact the company.
- Preserve the frontend's existing API boundaries and verify the actual quote behavior before promising submission.
- Keep navigation and forms usable on phones and with a keyboard. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the Clear Impression name, existing logo, and straightforward local service voice. Preserve verified business details and avoid inventing reviews, prices, guarantees, or coverage.

## Evidence on Hand

The frontend includes company imagery, review excerpts, service-area data, and the supplied appointment terms. Use these existing assets and facts when extending the site.
